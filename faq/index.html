01
FOUNDATIONS

Product

What is OhMoon?

OhMoon is an independent launch and discovery interface built around Pump.fun. It brings project preparation, token creation, a recent-launch board and a public survival framework into one consistent product.

Is OhMoon owned by or affiliated with Pump.fun?

No. OhMoon is an independent product. Pump.fun is the external launch and market layer used by the interface; its services, rules and availability remain separate.

Is OhMoon a token issuer, exchange or custodian?

OhMoon provides an interface. Your wallet authorizes transactions, Pump.fun provides the launch rail and Solana settles transactions. OhMoon does not hold your seed phrase or private key.

02
DISCOVERY

Live feed

Which tokens are included?

The board accepts network-wide new-token creation events from the Pump.fun pool only. It does not mix in general Solana tokens, unrelated DEX listings or manually selected projects.

How quickly does the board update?

A scheduled server job rebuilds the recent Pump.fun snapshot approximately every five minutes. The open page checks for the newest published snapshot every minute, so the normal visible delay is several minutes rather than tick-by-tick trading speed.

How are launches ordered?

Newest discovery time first. There is no paid placement or editorial ranking in the main creation feed. Search and filters only narrow the same underlying list.

Does appearing on OhMoon mean a token is verified?

No. Inclusion is automatic, not an endorsement, audit, quality check or recommendation. Names, images, links and descriptions come from public token metadata and may be misleading.

Why can an image, market cap or volume be missing?

The creation event arrives before some metadata and market services finish indexing. Remote image gateways can also be slow or unavailable. OhMoon shows placeholders or pending values instead of inventing data.

Why might a new Pump.fun token be delayed or absent?

Public streams, GitHub scheduling, metadata gateways and the user’s connection can all experience gaps. The feed is a best-effort recent window, not a permanent archive of every token ever created.

03
CREATION

Launching

What do I need to launch?

A compatible Solana wallet, enough SOL for the transaction and applicable fees, plus the token name, ticker, image, description and optional official links.

What happens after I press Launch?

OhMoon validates the form, uploads the image and metadata, prepares the Pump.fun token-creation transaction and asks the connected wallet to review and sign it. A token is not created merely by filling in the form.

Where is project metadata stored?

The launch workflow uploads public metadata through AR.IO Turbo-compatible storage before creating the token. Public metadata should be treated as permanent; do not include secrets or personal information.

What fees will I pay?

Solana network costs and Pump.fun or other platform fees may apply and can change. The complete transaction preview in your wallet is the source of truth—review it before signing.

Can OhMoon reverse or edit a launch?

No. Confirmed blockchain transactions cannot be undone by OhMoon. Public metadata may also be permanent. Verify spelling, image, links, mint details and wallet instructions before approval.

04
CONTINUITY

Survival ladder

What is the unlock ladder?

It is OhMoon’s public 0, 7, 30 and 90-day framework for making project age and continuity legible: Newborn, Alive, Proven and Matured.

Does the ladder lock funds or guarantee rewards?

Not currently. The displayed ladder is a product and information framework, not an on-chain escrow or guarantee. No reward should be treated as claimable unless a separate audited program and an actual wallet transaction prove it.

Does reaching a checkpoint prove a project is safe?

No. Age is not proof of liquidity, integrity, code quality or future performance. The checkpoints describe elapsed time and continuity only.

SECURITY

Safety & risk

Does OhMoon ever ask for my seed phrase?

No legitimate OhMoon flow needs it. Never type a seed phrase or private key into this website, a support message or a social account.

How should I verify a token before trading?

Check the full mint address, creator information, metadata links, wallet transaction instructions and independent on-chain data. A ticker or image can be copied by anyone.

Are market values and curve percentages exact?

They can be delayed estimates derived from creation events and third-party market data. Treat your wallet and current on-chain state as authoritative.

Is anything on OhMoon financial advice?

No. Token launches and meme-asset trading are highly speculative and can result in total loss. OhMoon presents tools and public information, not a promise of value or performance.

What is the official OhMoon account?

The official product account currently linked by the site is @ohmoonlaunch. Verify the domain and account before following instructions from social media.
