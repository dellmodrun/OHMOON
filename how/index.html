THE OHMOON PROTOCOL

From first click
to day ninety.

OhMoon is an independent interface built around Pump.fun launches. It combines launch preparation, a network-wide creation feed and a transparent survival framework without taking custody of your wallet.

You review and approve transactions in your wallet. OhMoon never receives your seed phrase or private key.

RESPONSIBILITY SPLIT

What each layer does

OhMoonInterface, launch form, metadata workflow, discovery feed and survival presentation.
Pump.funToken creation rail, bonding-curve market and its own platform rules and fees.
Solana walletIdentity, transaction review, signature approval and custody of funds.
Third-party dataCreation events and market estimates that can be delayed or temporarily unavailable.
DATA PIPELINE

How the board stays current

  1. A scheduled server job reads the recent Pump.fun token list and, when configured, the PumpPortal new-token stream.
  2. Only creation records identified as belonging to the Pump.fun pool enter the board.
  3. The public repository snapshot refreshes approximately every five minutes.
  4. Metadata and market estimates load separately, so images or values can arrive later than the creation event.

BEFORE YOU CONTINUE

Read the transaction, not the marketing.

Token launches and trading are high-risk. Feed inclusion is automatic and is not verification. Market cap, curve progress and volume can be estimates. Always verify the mint address and the complete wallet transaction before signing.
