OhMoon does not replace Pump.fun. It adds a transparent reward schedule between the creator and the creator rewards generated by the coin.

01

Create the coin on Pump.fun

The coin uses Pump.fun’s own launch flow, bonding curve and PumpSwap graduation. The creator signs the launch with their own wallet.

creator→Pump.fun→coin
02

Route creator rewards to the Survival Vault

The launch registers a public reward recipient and a fixed 90-day schedule. The schedule cannot be presented as instant creator income.

trades→creator rewards→vault
03

Unlock by surviving

The creator’s claimable share moves through four checkpoints: 0% at launch, 20% on day 7, 50% on day 30 and 100% on day 90.

0%→20%→50%→100%
Important: OhMoon is an independent interface and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pump.fun. The official Pump.fun Developer API is read-only; the beta therefore sends users to Pump.fun to authorize coin creation. Vault enrollment and any reward routing must be clearly shown and separately authorized.
