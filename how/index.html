our own platform.
better launch.

launch runs its own platform on Raydium LaunchLab. Every coin is launched on it from one master wallet, trades on its bonding curve right here, and graduates to a Raydium pool. What it changes is who launches, what is allowed, and where the fees go: back into the coin, into $LAUNCH, and into the fire.

01

one master wallet.

Every coin is created by the same wallet, the platform's treasury, which pays the rent. No dev wallet per coin and no dev bag to dump. Every buyback comes from that same wallet, so it shows on the chart as the dev buying. Launching is free.

02

you pick the pair.

Each coin's bonding curve is quoted in a pair you choose at launch: SOL, a tokenized stock like SPYx, NVDAx or QQQx, USDC, or any of 580 pairs. Every pair gets the same curve: about $2.8k to start and about $41k to graduate, the raise sized in the pair's own units. Your coin is a bet on its pair too.

03

no vamps.

Before the master wallet signs anything: the ticker must be new (case, spaces, lookalike letters and leetspeak fold into one), the picture can't be a resized or recoloured copy, and an X account owns one coin. Names are yours to pick. A ticker already trading elsewhere with real money is refused too. The database enforces it, so two launches racing for one ticker can't both win.

04

no block 0.

Snipers win the first block by riding in the creator's bundle or by seeing the create before it lands. Here the create goes out as a private bundle with a tip, never through the public pool, and the mint address is never published early. Nobody can be in the block it is created in. After that it trades like any coin on its curve: no same-block edge, no promise of sniper-proof.

05

every trade burns twice.

Every trade on every coin pays the platform a 3% fee (Raydium takes its own 0.25% on top). Every few minutes the master wallet claims it and splits it three ways: 1% buys the coin that was traded back and burns it, 1% buys $LAUNCH and burns it, and 1% stays in the treasury. Each coin's share comes from its own trades, so the ledger shows exactly what it earned and what it burned. The buybacks go through Jupiter on the open market, on the curve or the Raydium pool after graduation, and every token bought is burned. Launches never spend buyback money.
